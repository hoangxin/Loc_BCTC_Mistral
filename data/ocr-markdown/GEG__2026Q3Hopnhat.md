**- THE HO CHI MINH STOCK EXCHANGE**

Pursuant to the regulations on information disclosure according to Circular No. 96/2020/TT-BTC dated November 16, 2020, of the Ministry of Finance, Gia Lai Electricity Joint Stock Company (stock symbol: GEG) hereby provides the following explanation for the variance of over 10% in its profit after corporate income tax (CIT) in the Income Statement for Quarter 3/2026 compared to the same period last year:

*Unit: billion VND*

|  No | Item | Quarter 3/2026 | Quarter 3/2025 | Change Q3/2026-Q3/2025  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1 | Profit after CIT in Separate Financial Statements | 38.04 | 73.85 | (35.81)  |
|  2 | Profit after CIT in Consolidated Financial Statements | 100.42 | 80.06 | 20.36  |

**1.** Profit after corporate income tax, according to the Separate Income Statement for Q3 2026, reached VND 38.04 billion, representing a decrease of VND 35.81 billion, or 48%, compared to the same period last year, due to the following reasons:

- Revenue decreased by over VND 79 billion, of which revenue from sales of electricity dropped by nearly VND 29 billion,
- Financial income decreased by more than VND 11 billion, mainly attributed to lower dividend income received from subsidiaries.

**2.** Profit after corporate income tax, according to the Consolidated Income Statement for Q3 2026, reached VND 100.42 billion, representing an increase of VND 20.36 billion, or 25%, compared to the same period last year, mainly due to an increase of VND 15.1 billion in electricity revenue.

Above is the explanation of Gia Lai Electricity Joint Stock Company on the variance in profit after CIT on the Income Statement for Q3 2026 compared to the same period last year.
